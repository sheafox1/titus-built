Our Story

A name on the door

Jeff Titus launched Titus Built in 1995 with a degree in construction engineering and a background that included an architecture firm, a residential builder and the construction division of an energy services company. His family came to Fairfield County in 1918, and he says putting his name on the business signifies commitment, community, integrity and trust.
